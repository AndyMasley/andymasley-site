Phase gates

Gates are their own sequence position, not homework stacked on a week. PLAN.md's buffer weeks (one unscheduled consolidation week per ~2 phases) are the gate weeks.

How to run one. Retrieval bank first, closed book, one sitting, written out rather than recited. Then the transfer prompt, open book, written so the answer could ship as a blog post. A failed item routes to the named week, not to the whole phase. Score with the rubric.

Phase I gate · orientation and technical repair (W0 to W6)

Retrieval, closed book.

  1. Write the system stack's eight levels and the claim ladder's five rungs.
  2. What do a matrix's columns tell you, what does rank count, and what does a projection discard?
  3. State Eckart-Young. What does the truncated SVD promise, and what does it not promise about task-relevant directions?
  4. Define cross-entropy loss in one sentence. Give perplexity's relation to it.
  5. Why is lower cross-entropy not closer to truth? Name both limits.
  6. What single question does the backward sweep answer, what does it cost against the forward pass, and what does it pay in?
  7. Derive the ~6 FLOPs per parameter per token estimate from that cost.
  8. Name the nine things that must hold for a reproduction, and say which one a seed pins.

Transfer. Write 800 words on what a compute threshold counts. Take Article 51's 10^25 presumption and SB 53's 10^26 test, and show which drafting choices (backward-pass FLOPs, discarded runs, fine-tuning, precision) move real models across the line.

Pass: 6 of 8, plus a transfer draft a stranger could read. Remediation: items 2 and 3 route to W2 and W3 at full speed, then redo the W0 gateway checks 4 and 5; items 4 and 5 to W4; items 6 and 7 to W5; item 8 to W6.

Phase II gate · learning systems (W7 to W11)

Retrieval, closed book.

  1. Decompose test error into three parts. Which does weight decay attack, and what does it pay?
  2. Name the three assumptions behind "it generalizes, we tested on held-out data," none checkable from the score.
  3. Why do two stacked linear layers collapse, and what does a tanh gradient of 1 − a² imply about saturated units?
  4. State the ResNet evidence that capacity was never the bottleneck.
  5. Why does a per-layer scale factor compound, and what do initialization and normalization each do about it?
  6. Write Var(A−B) for a paired comparison and say what pairing cancels.
  7. What does item clustering do to effective sample size and to the naive standard error?
  8. Define calibration as a frequency promise. What does temperature scaling leave untouched?
  9. Distinguish E[Y | A=1] from E[Y^{a=1}], and give the condition under which they coincide.

Transfer. Write the four lines that make an eval score mean something in a contract: a standard error on every headline number, paired designs for model-vs-model claims, disclosed seeds and sampling settings, a power analysis. Show on one worked example what each line rules out.

Pass: 7 of 9. Remediation: items 1 and 2 to W7; 3 to W8; 4 and 5 to W9; 6 and 7 to W10; 8 and 9 to W11.

Phase III gate · transformers (W12 to W18)

Retrieval, closed book.

  1. Factor a language model's distribution by the chain rule and connect the training objective to W4's cross-entropy.
  2. Rebuild scaled dot-product attention from memory. Give the shapes in Q·Kᵀ/√d and say why the mask goes before the softmax.
  3. Which of Q, K, V cache across generated tokens, and what does that make long context?
  4. In a GPT-2 block, which carries more parameters, attention or the MLP? Which operation moves information between positions?
  5. Why did pre-norm win over post-norm?
  6. List what "the model works" needs to be a measurement claim. Give the arithmetic baseline for a 65-character vocabulary.
  7. Show how exact match on a k-token answer turns a continuous per-token gain into a sharp benchmark jump.
  8. For a sparse-expert model, which number governs memory and which governs FLOPs per token?
  9. Temperature against top-p: what does each do to the distribution, and what four items belong in a decoding disclosure line?

Transfer. Write the general-audience piece on why an attention heatmap is not an explanation. Use the routing mechanism, the ablation test, and the fact that a human picked the head. Close on what evidence counts instead.

Pass: 7 of 9, and item 2 is mandatory. Remediation: failing the shape trace sends you back to W13's build before anything else; item 5 to W14; item 6 to W15 with W4; item 7 to W16; items 8 and 9 to W17 and W18.

Phase IV gate · training and serving at scale (W19 to W23)

Retrieval, closed book.

  1. Give the operational definition of data quality, and say why a serious corpus is a stack of ablation results.
  2. Why can provenance not be retrofitted? Name three things you cannot do without it.
  3. Write L(N, D) = E + A/N^α + B/D^β and say which term is not the model's to reduce.
  4. Why is Chinchilla-optimal not how frontier models are trained?
  5. Define arithmetic intensity and a chip's ridge point. Which regime is batch-1 decoding in?
  6. Distinguish TWh from GW, and say which question each answers.
  7. Name Epoch's three confidence tiers, and what a bare point estimate has silently claimed.
  8. For data, tensor, pipeline, and ZeRO sharding: what is sharded, and what collective is owed on which link?
  9. Why is parameter count not a price? Give both failure modes.

Transfer. Write 1,000 words on why a public training-compute number is a reconstruction rather than a measurement. Walk one estimate through the unknowns, attach Epoch's tier, and state what a reporting rule would have to fix: unit, route, audit.

Pass: 7 of 9. Remediation: items 1 and 2 to W19; 3 and 4 to W20; 5 to 7 to W21; 8 to W22; 9 to W23 with W18's KV-cache arithmetic.

Phase V gate · posttraining and reasoning (W24 to W28)

Retrieval, closed book.

  1. What is the unit of evidence for a fine-tune?
  2. What does LoRA cut, and what does it leave untouched?
  3. Why is a preference label not a readout of human values? Give InstructGPT's labeler agreement figure.
  4. Under Bradley-Terry, why is comparing raw reward scores across prompts a category error?
  5. Map RLHF onto an MDP: state, action, policy, and when reward arrives.
  6. Under one gradient step per batch, what happens to PPO's clipping?
  7. State DPO's implicit reward. What does deleting the RL loop fail to delete?
  8. Describe the proxy-against-gold divergence Gao et al. measured, and why best-of-n makes the point sharper.
  9. What does a verifiable reward buy, and what stays as unconstrained as before?
  10. Why does test-time compute stress a training-compute threshold?

Transfer. Write the posttraining disclosure line: whose preferences, under what instructions, aggregated by what rule, optimized against what reward with what leash. Then argue whether Annex XI's "training methodologies and techniques" already reaches it.

Phase VI gate · retrieval, tools, agents (W29 to W32)

Retrieval, closed book.

  1. Name the four separable joints where a retrieval pipeline fails, and the two joints where grounding gets measured.
  2. Give Liu et al.'s support and citation figures, and the sign of the correlation between citation precision and perceived utility.
  3. Why does obligation attach to the system rather than to the weights?
  4. What changes when the capability-defining event is a connection rather than a model release?
  5. Define the confused-deputy failure, and say why a system prompt is not a permission system.
  6. Give AgentDojo's utility-against-attack numbers for one defense, and state the isolation floor.
  7. Name the three moving parts of a task horizon, and what METR's 50% figure means.
  8. What happens to measured horizons at an 80% success bar?
  9. Name the two directions in which broken benchmark tests and contamination bias a score.

Transfer. A solicitation says "responses shall cite source documents." Write the acceptance criteria that line fails to buy: claim-level citation precision and recall on the agency's own corpus, plus a measured refusal rate on out-of-corpus queries. Score one shipping product against them.

Phase IX gate · safety, security, systemic risk (W41 to W45)

This gate carries the frontier-system audit. Budget it like a week, not like a quiz.

Retrieval, closed book.

  1. Write the chain between a capability score and a body. Define uplift as a counterfactual.
  2. Why is a high hazardous-knowledge score not evidence of uplift? Which way does the inference run?
  3. Give RAND's red-cell result and the effect size that design could have detected.
  4. Where do Anthropic's RSP and OpenAI's Preparedness Framework part on the bio threshold?
  5. What is a model organism? What does construction buy, and what does it cost?
  6. Which number in the alignment-faking work carries the evidential weight, and why not the raw rate?
  7. Distinguish alignment from control. What unmeasurable question does the red team convert, and into what?
  8. When does the control window close?
  9. Why is "secure" not a property? What does an OC index add to a security claim?
  10. What earns a risk the word "systemic," and what makes a harm merely large?

The audit. Pick the newest system card on the roster page. Sources: the OpenAI Deployment Safety Hub, Anthropic system cards, DeepMind model cards.

Build the claim ledger first. One row per consequential claim in the card: the claim, its rung on the claim ladder, the evidence offered, the population and elicitation settings behind it, what it cannot establish, and what would check it.

Then write a five-page assessment covering both halves.

  • Capabilities, on W36 to W40 judgment. Which scores state a construct, an elicitation protocol, and an uncertainty. Which benchmarks are contaminated or retired. Whether each number attaches to the model or to model-plus-scaffold. Whether a trend claim states resolution criteria.
  • Safety posture, on W41 to W44. Which capability thresholds fire and what they trigger. Whether uplift claims name a baseline. Whether deception or test-awareness evidence appears. Whether the security standard names an attacker class. What control evidence exists, as against assertion.

Done when every ledger row cites a page or line in the card, the assessment names three claims you would not sign and the evidence that would change that, and a named principal could act on page one.

Pass: 8 of 10 on the bank, plus an assessment that survives its own source-scope-uncertainty audit. Remediation: items 1 to 4 to W41; 5 and 6 to W42; 7 and 8 to W43; 9 to W44; 10 to W45.

If the weak part was grading the card's evidence rather than reading its safety case, the failure is Phase VIII. Rerun W36 and W37 before rewriting.

  3. Name the four commitments that price a threat model.
